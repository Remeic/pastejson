# Privacy Policy — pastejson

Last updated: August 26, 2026

## Who we are

pastejson is a free online JSON formatter operated by Giulio Fagioli ([justgiulio.dev](https://justgiulio.dev)) as data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## What happens to your data

Everything you paste, type or drop into the page is processed **100% locally in your browser**. Your JSON is never uploaded, transmitted, stored or logged by us. Closing the tab erases it.

## Data we do not collect

- No cookies
- No analytics or tracking scripts
- No account, no forms, no identifiers
- No third-party network requests while you use the tool

## Hosting logs

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. As with any web server, Vercel may process standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamp) in server logs for security and abuse prevention. This processing is based on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We do not access or use these logs for profiling.

## Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–21)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, most rights are satisfied trivially: there is nothing to disclose or delete. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.
